THE ASTR VALUATION MODEL

A defined adjustment formula.

Astrality uses the number of buyers and an item's ASTR price to calculate the increase in its internal valuation adjustment.

Increase in percentage points = (number of buyers ÷ 4) × item price in ASTR

For example, one buyer purchasing a 28 ASTR item produces an adjustment increase of 7 percentage points.

The welcome bonus can also affect the adjustment if the backend treats it as a qualifying event. Under the same formula, a qualifying 100 ASTR credit with one buyer would add 25 percentage points.

How the displayed USD value is calculated.

USD value = (ASTR balance ÷ 2.5) × (1 + adjustment percentage ÷ 100)

For example, 25 ASTR with a 7% adjustment displays an illustrative value of $10.70.

These are internal accounting rules. They do not independently establish market demand, a redeemable USD price, or guaranteed investment returns. Actual balances and adjustments must be calculated and stored by the server.
